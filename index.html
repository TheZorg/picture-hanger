    Formula

    Nail Height = Center + (Frame Height / 2) − Anchor Drop

    Example: 24 in tall frame, 2 in wire drop, 57 in center → 57 + 12 − 2 = 67 in.

    57–60 in Eye-Level Rule

    Keep artwork center 57–60 in off the finished floor. Use 57 in in hallways or low-ceiling rooms; use 60 in for large pieces or tall ceilings.

    Anchor Drop

    Wire hangers: Pull the wire taut upward with a tape measure and measure from the peak to the top edge of the frame.
    Sawtooth / D-rings: Measure the fixed distance from the top edge to the tooth or ring.

    How to hang a picture frame at eye level (FAQ)

    What is the 57-inch rule? Museums and galleries center artwork at 57–60 inches from the floor, which matches average human eye level. Use 57 inches for smaller rooms and hallways, or 60 inches for tall ceilings and large statement pieces.

    How does the calculator find nail height? It adds half of your frame height to your target center height to locate the top edge of the frame, then subtracts the anchor drop so the nail lands at the exact hanging point.
